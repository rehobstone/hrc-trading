Who we are

A Nigerian trading house built for international expectations.

HRC Trading Limited operates at the point where extraction meets export. We source solid minerals from verified Nigerian origins, prepare them to buyer specification, and manage the documentation and logistics that move them across borders.

Alongside minerals, we handle the import and export of general goods — matching suppliers to buyers and keeping the commercial and regulatory chain intact from purchase order to delivery.

How we work

From enquiry to delivered cargo.

A clear sequence, agreed in writing before material moves — so both sides know what is being delivered, when, and against which documents.

01

Enquiry & specification

You state the mineral or goods, grade, volume, destination and required delivery terms.

02

Sampling & offer

We confirm availability, arrange sampling or inspection, and issue a written offer with pricing and Incoterms.

03

Contract & compliance

Contract signed, payment instrument agreed, export permits and origin documentation put in motion.

04

Logistics & delivery

Standards & compliance

Paperwork is not an afterthought.

Cross-border commodity trade fails on documentation more often than on supply. We treat the compliance chain as part of the product — prepared before cargo is committed, not assembled afterwards.

Buyers receive a complete document set with each shipment, and can appoint their own inspection agent at loading.

  • 01
    Mineral title & supplier verificationConfirming the legal basis of the material before purchase.
  • 02
    Export permits & Nigerian regulatory clearanceHandled through the appropriate mineral and customs authorities.
  • 03
    Certificate of OriginIssued through the recognised chamber and export channels.
  • 04
    Independent inspectionThird-party quality and quantity certification at buyer's option.
  • 05
    Shipping & trade documentsBill of lading, packing lists, commercial invoice, insurance.
